Grandson settings

This page is usually filled in once, by a family member. After that, click the Grandson picture at the top of the browser whenever you want help with a page.

1. The key that lets Grandson explain pages

Grandson uses Claude, made by Anthropic, to understand pages. That needs an Anthropic API key, which you can create at console.anthropic.com. Each explanation costs a few cents, charged to that account. The scam warnings work without a key.

2. Who to call when unsure

If Grandson spots a scam, it will suggest calling this person before doing anything. This is saved only in this browser and is never sent anywhere.

3. Reading aloud

What Grandson sees and sends

The scam warnings are worked out inside this browser. Nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere for that.

Only when you press Explain this page, Is this page safe? or Ask, Grandson sends a picture of the visible part of that page, the page's words and its web address to Anthropic so Claude can answer. If the page shows private things, such as a bank balance, they are in that picture. Words typed into password boxes are not readable in it.

Grandson can only look and explain. It cannot click, type, buy, or send anything.
